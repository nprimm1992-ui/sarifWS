# Intelligence Substrate Economics

This Praxis entry documents a core operating principle of the Sarif methodology — written for operators who need **traceable reasoning**, not narrative polish.

## The structural claim

Most consulting artifacts optimize for consumption speed. Sarif optimizes for **coherence under adversarial review**: every recommendation must remain connected to the evidence that produced it.
